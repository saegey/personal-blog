Pre-Race Setup
	1.	How did you approach warm-up for this short, intense climb compared to a longer gravel race? Did you feel properly primed when the clock started?
	    - It was neutral rollout from start and a pretty longer staggered wait letting the riders so I was already kinda cooled down from earlier. Legs felt pretty good from what i can remember even after the long 50 miles stage the day before.
	2.	Did you make any specific gear or tire choices for the steep climbing and gravel surface?
	    - Not really, I didnt change wheels to a 700c pathfinder pro 42 from the day before When I used a 650B WTB Sendero knobby tire for the mountain trails. I gelled up before the start and made sure I was fueled. I was unsure of how much elevation would effect me so I might have had more effort but I didn't want to blow part of the way up the climb.

Early Climb / Rollout
	3.	The neutral rollout is unusual for a time trial — did it affect your pacing or mindset once the official start began? It was kinda cool to ride next to payson mcelveen and such in the stage. The wait kinda sucks some but not much you can do about that.
	4.	How did you manage your pacing in the opening minutes — did you go out aggressively or settle into target power? - I started hard in the beginning and settle in to a good tempo. I am strong climber and have done well in the past with pacing by feel.

Mid-Climb
	5.	With altitude topping out at ~8500ft, did you notice your breathing or power fade differently than at sea level? - not anything extreme but I could feel the effort more in my legs and lungs.
	6.	How did the heat (80°F average) and smoke impact you physically or mentally while climbing?
	   - Since the effort was quite short (20 min) i didn't feel the heat too much. I was more focused on the effort and the climb itself. The smoke was a bit of a concern but it didn't seem to affect my performance significantly.

Key Moments
	7.	Was there a point during the ~29 minutes where you had to consciously back off or, conversely, where you dug especially deep to maintain momentum?
		- no, I actually was passing a lot of others and it help motivate me to push harder.
	8.	Did you encounter any technical challenges on the gravel — traction, line choice, or handling — or was it more of a pure watts-to-weight effort?
		- gravel was really pretty good and it was easy to find a good line and push hard without worrying about traction. I was able to focus on my power and effort without worrying about the terrain too much. The climb also didnt contain any crazy steep ramps.

Finish & Aftermath
	9.	How did you feel crossing the line — more like a clean execution of your plan, or did you leave something on the table?
	   - I felt good with the effort and gained some time back on some friends I had there. 26 out of 138 on the day and gained some time back on GC after losing some on the stage the day before. I was happy with my performance and felt like I executed my plan well.
	10.	Looking back, is there anything you would adjust (pacing, warm-up, gear, nutrition/hydration beforehand) if you raced this climb again?
	   - Not really, this was actually a pretty solid execution of my plan. I felt well-prepared and didn't encounter any major issues.
